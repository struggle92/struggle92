# Print-on-Demand: Night Shift Nurse Collection

**10 designs × 2 ink colors** (`designs/*-dark.png` for light shirts, `*-light.png` for dark shirts).
4500×5400 px transparent PNG at 300 DPI, which is Printful's and Printify's front-print spec. All in `pod-designs.zip`.
The fonts are SIL Open Font License, so they're fine for commercial use.

## Before you list: a 2-minute trademark check (required)

Search each phrase at **tmsearch.uspto.gov** (live marks, class 025 = clothing). Short phrases sometimes get trademarked, and Etsy and Amazon take down listings that use them. Skip any phrase that shows a live registration in class 025.

## Setup (Printify, free plan)

1. Go to printify.com → sign up → **Connect store** → choose **Shopify** (your store) and/or **Etsy**.
2. Pick a product: the **Bella+Canvas 3001** tee (or Gildan 18000 for sweatshirts). Choose a US print provider with a 4.5★+ rating.
3. Upload the `-dark.png` file for white, heather and pastel colors. Upload the `-light.png` file for black, navy and charcoal.
4. Printify makes the mockup photos for you. Publish → it pushes the listing to Shopify or Etsy with variants.
5. Orders route to Printify automatically. You pay the base cost when an order comes in.

## How this gets sales (POD only works with volume and visibility)

- **Etsy search is the free traffic.** List all 10 designs × tee + sweatshirt = 20 listings in week 1. Etsy rewards shops with more listings and regular new ones.
- **Nurse Week is May 6–12.** It's the biggest sales spike of the year. Have everything live by mid-April.
- **Bulk unit orders:** offer "matching shirts for your unit" with a 10%-off code for 6+ shirts. Nurses order as teams.
- **Do not** use hospital logos, brand names (like Starbucks cups), or anything trademarked. Do not claim "handmade". Etsy requires print-on-demand items to list the production partner (Printify's print provider) in the listing settings.
