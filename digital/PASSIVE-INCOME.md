# Passive Income Plan (ranked by what you can actually do now)

"Passive" means **build once, sell automatically.** It's never zero work: each idea needs traffic, which means content, and that's the part you keep doing. Ranked by how fast it can earn × how little upkeep it needs.

## 1. Digital products in your Shopify store (BUILT)

| Product | Price | Status | Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Faceless Shorts Playbook (9-page PDF) | $27 | Draft. Attach the PDF, then publish | Automatic download |
| No-Pick Night Routine + 30-Day Tracker (5-page PDF) | $7 | Draft. Attach the PDF, then publish | Automatic download |

- **Margin:** ~95% (only Shopify payment fees). No supplier and no shipping.
- **The tracker** is a $7 add-on for patch buyers: offer it in the cart and the order confirmation email. Getting even 1 in 5 patch buyers to add it raises your average order with zero extra cost.
- **The playbook** already has a sales page (`funnel/index.html#digital`), ads and an email sequence (`copy/digital.md`). Put the product link in that sales page. Promote it with the faceless videos you'd make anyway: the product *is* the method you're using.
- It doesn't fit a skincare store's look, so don't put it in the main menu. Sell it through its direct product link only.

## 2. Affiliate content (no inventory, no customer service)

You already have a bridge page (`funnel/index.html#affiliate`). The faceless Shorts in the playbook are the traffic engine.
- **Amazon Associates** for tools you'd mention anyway (ring lights, phone tripods, skincare basics).
- **TikTok Shop affiliate**: tag other sellers' products in your videos and earn commission. You need a follower minimum, so check the current number in the app.
- **Software affiliates** (CapCut Pro, Canva Pro and similar) pay recurring commission. Join through each brand's official program page.
- Always disclose: #ad or "affiliate link".

## 3. Print-on-demand (Printful or Printify + Shopify)

A design goes on a mug, tee or phone case, and the supplier prints and ships each order. Zero inventory. Margins are thinner (25–40%). It works best with a niche joke or identity ("night shift nurse", local city pride). It plugs into the same store setup. **Next step if you want it:** pick 1 niche and I'll design 10 products.

## 4. Templates (Etsy + Shopify)

Canva templates (social post packs, resume templates, budget planners, wedding signs) sell well on Etsy, where buyers already search. Etsy charges a small fee per listing, so check its current pricing. Make it once and sell it forever. **Next step if you want it:** I build a 30-template "small business social pack" PDF + Canva links.

## 5. YouTube Shorts / long-form ad revenue (slowest, but compounds)

Faceless channel → YouTube Partner Program once you meet the requirements (check YouTube's official page for the current thresholds). Old videos keep earning for years. Pair it with #1 and #2.

## What I would NOT do

- **"Passive" crypto, forex or trading bots, and AI "money printing" apps:** mostly losses or scams.
- **Reselling PLR/MRR bundles with income claims:** saturated, and it gets ad accounts banned.
- **Buying followers or fake reviews:** platforms detect it, and your reach dies.

## Weekly upkeep (about 3 hours a week once it's set up)

1. Batch 7 videos (Sunday, about 2 hours).
2. Check sales and top posts (15 minutes). Make more versions of the winners.
3. Answer customer emails (15 minutes).
4. Add 1 new product or template a month.
