# Etsy Printables: Listing Kit

Each product is an instant digital download. Upload the ZIP (US Letter + A4 PDFs inside) as the digital file. **Cover photos** are in `listing-photos/`. Add 3–4 more photos by screenshotting single pages from the PDFs.

## Setup

1. etsy.com/sell → open your shop (Etsy charges a small one-time setup fee and a per-listing fee; check the current amounts on the sign-up page).
2. Listing → **Type: Digital** → upload `downloads/<name>.zip`.
3. Category: *Paper & Party Supplies → Paper → Calendars & Planners*.
4. "Who made it": **I did**. "What is it": **A finished product**. "When was it made": **2020–2026**.
5. Renewal: automatic. Etsy's algorithm favors listings that renew.

## How printables get found

- **Listing count matters.** Make color variations (sage, terracotta, blue, black-and-white) of each planner and list each as its own listing. Change the `--a` and `--bg` colors at the top of each file in `src/` and re-render. That turns 3 products into 12–16 listings.
- **January** (budget and planners) and **August** (back-to-school) are peak months.
- Pin each cover image to Pinterest with a link to the listing. Pinterest drives a lot of printable traffic for free.
