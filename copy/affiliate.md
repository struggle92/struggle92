# Offer 3: Affiliate bridge page (example: Canva Pro)

**Before anything else:** apply to Canva's affiliate program (Canva's affiliates page, usually run through Impact). Once you're approved, replace `afterLink` in `funnel/page.html` with your personal tracking link. Until then the page links to Canva's normal Pro page and you earn nothing.

You can swap in any real program you actually use, such as CapCut, Hostinger, ConvertKit or Amazon Associates. Edit the `affiliate` block in the page config.

Rules that keep your accounts alive:
- Keep the affiliate disclosure on the page and in every post ("#ad" or "affiliate link" in the caption).
- Only claim features the tool really has, and check them against its current pricing page.
- Don't use the brand's logo as if you are the company.

## VSL script (about 5 min, screen recording)

"Here's how I make a whole week of posts in one hour." Show: duplicate the template set, swap text, Magic Resize, schedule. "That's Canva Pro. There's a free trial, so try it on your own content this week. My link's below. I may earn a commission if you sign up, and it doesn't cost you anything extra. Put your email in and I'll send you the template pack I just used."

## Ads / posts

1. "One design → 6 platform sizes in 90 seconds. Here's my Canva setup (affiliate link, free trial)."
2. "The 7 templates I reuse every week. Free pack, link in bio. #ad"
3. Before/after: messy Story vs. a branded one. "Brand Kit does this automatically."

## Short-form scripts

1. "Stop resizing designs by hand." Demo Magic Resize. "Link in bio, free trial. #ad"
2. "My one-hour content week." Timelapse.
3. "Free Canva vs. Pro: the 3 features that actually matter for creators."

## Email sequence (template pack opt-in)

1. **Your template pack** — Link to your shared Canva templates, plus the trial link with disclosure.
2. **How to use template #1** — A 3-step tutorial.
3. **The resize trick** — A GIF or short video.
4. **Trial ending reminder** (only if they told you they started one) — How to cancel or keep it. Being straight here builds trust.
