# Offer 1: Done-for-you short videos for local businesses ($497/mo)

## VSL script (about 4 min, film on your phone, talking head plus screen recordings)

**Hook (0:00–0:15)**
"If you own a local business, there's a shop in your town getting calls from TikTok and Reels right now. They aren't better than you. They just post every day. In the next four minutes I'll show you how to do the same thing with 20 minutes of filming a week."

**Problem (0:15–1:00)**
"Most owners know they should post. But you're running the business. Editing takes hours, you don't know what to film, and the few videos you've posted got 40 views. So you stop. Meanwhile your competitor posts a 15-second before-and-after every day, and people see their name 30 times before they need the service."

**Show it (1:00–2:15)**
[Screen record: a raw 60-second clip, then the edited 15-second version with captions and hook.]
"This is the raw clip an owner sent me. Here's what we turned it into. Hook in the first second, captions, one clear message, and a line telling people how to book. We do this 12 times a month and post it everywhere: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube."

**How it works (2:15–3:00)**
"Step one, a free 15-minute audit where I show you the first three videos I'd make for you. Step two, every Monday you get a shot list. Film it on your phone, upload it, and we edit within 48 hours. Step three, we post it and send you a monthly report showing views, profile visits and messages."

**Offer and close (3:00–4:00)**
"It's $497 a month with no contract. I take five new businesses a month so turnaround stays fast. Tap the button under this video, book the free audit, and even if you never hire me you'll leave with three video ideas you can film tomorrow."

## TikTok / Shorts / Reels scripts (film yourself)

1. **"I edited a plumber's 60-second clip into this."** Show raw, then edited. End: "If you own a local business and want this every day, link in bio."
2. **"3 videos every local business should post this week."** (1) before/after, (2) a 10-second answer to your most common customer question, (3) the owner's face saying the price range. Close: "Want me to make these for you? Link in bio."
3. **"Why your business page gets 40 views."** Hook is too slow, no captions, no call to action. Show the fix on screen.

(Check each group's rules before posting. Many ban self-promo outside set days.)

## Email follow-up (after audit request)

1. **Subject: Your audit times** — Two slots, ask for their page link, one sentence on what they'll get.
2. **Subject: The 3 videos I'd make first** (send after the call) — Recap ideas, a link to one example, the $497 offer and a reply-to-start line.
3. **Subject: What 12 videos a month looks like** — A calendar screenshot and an example month for a similar business.
4. **Subject: "I hate being on camera"** — Handle the top objection: faceless formats, hands-only, voiceover.
5. **Subject: Closing this month's spots** — True scarcity only: say how many of the 5 spots are left. Final call to reply.
