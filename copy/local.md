# Offer 4: Local business (example: mobile car detailing)

Swap in your real business: pressure washing, junk removal, lawn care, cleaning or furniture flipping. Edit the `local` block in `funnel/page.html`. Set real prices, your ZIP codes and your business name before you share it.

## VSL (90 sec, vertical, doubles as your main ad)

0:00 Close-up of the dirtiest part of the car. Text: "3 years of kids + coffee + dog hair."
0:05 Timelapse of the detail.
0:60 The reveal, slow pan.
0:75 You on camera: "I come to your driveway, bring my own water and power, and text you photos when it's done. Sedans from $149. Tap the link for a quote."

## Facebook Marketplace post (under Services)

**Title:** Mobile Car Detailing – We Come To You – From $149
**Description:**
> Full interior + exterior detail in your driveway or work parking lot.
> ✔ Vacuum + shampoo seats and carpets
> ✔ Hand wash + spray wax, tires, glass
> ✔ Own water and power, nothing needed from you
> ✔ Before/after photos texted when done
> Sedans from $149 · SUVs/trucks from $189 · Pet hair +$30
> Serving [YOUR CITY / ZIPs]. Message me your year/make/model for a quote in under an hour.

**Photos:** 1 before/after split as the cover, 3 interior shots, 1 of you with the van or setup.
**Repost cadence:** renew every 7 days. Post a fresh listing with new photos every 2 weeks.

## Ads

1. **Before/after carousel:** "Your car. Your driveway. 3 hours. From $149. Get a quote."
2. **Local callout:** "[CITY] parents: we deep-clean minivans in your driveway. Goldfish crackers included at no extra charge."
3. **Offer:** "First-time customers: free pet-hair removal this month." Only run this if you're really offering it.

## TikTok / Shorts / Reels (post daily, these bring the calls)

1. The dirtiest car of the week, full timelapse.
2. "How much does a mobile detail cost in [CITY]?" Say your prices on camera.
3. A satisfying 10-second clip of the seat shampoo extraction, no talking.
4. Customer reaction (with permission).

## Text/email follow-up

1. **Instant:** "Hi [name], thanks for reaching out about your [vehicle]. Full detail is $[price]. I have [day/time] or [day/time]. Which works?"
2. **+24h if no reply:** "Still want to get the [vehicle] cleaned? Happy to send a couple of photos of similar jobs."
3. **After the job:** "Thanks! Here are your photos. Would you leave a quick Google review? [link]"
4. **+30 days:** "Maintenance wash for past customers this month is $[price]. Want a spot?"
