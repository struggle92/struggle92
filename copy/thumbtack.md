## 4. FAQs (Thumbtack asks these; paste the answers)

**What should the customer know about your pricing (e.g., discounts, fees)?**
> Standard jobs have flat prices, listed on my profile. Anything outside a flat-rate job is $65/hr with a 1-hour minimum. Materials are extra and you see the receipt. Bigger or unusual jobs get a free quote first. Monthly plans (yard, pet waste, home care) get priority booking and 10% off extra work.

**What is your typical process for working with a new customer?**
> 1. You message me with what needs doing and a photo if you can.
> 2. I reply with a flat price or a quote and a couple of open times.
> 3. I show up when I said I would, do the job and clean up.
> 4. I text you a photo of the finished work. You pay when you're happy with it.

**What education and/or training do you have that relates to your work?**
> Write one or two true sentences: years doing this kind of work, jobs you've done for family or employers, any certifications. Don't stretch it.

**How did you get started doing this type of work?**
> Write it in your own words, 2–3 sentences. Real stories win jobs: who taught you, the first job you got paid for, why you started the business.

**What types of customers have you worked with?**
> Homeowners, renters, busy parents, seniors who want a reliable person for small repairs, and landlords who need quick turnovers.

**Describe a recent project you are fundamentally proud of.**
> Use a real job with before/after photos. Example format: "A Kannapolis homeowner had a 65-inch TV sitting on the floor for two months. I mounted it, hid the cables in a raceway and hung two shelves in the same visit, done in 90 minutes."

**What advice would you give a customer looking to hire a provider in your area of work?**
> Get the price in writing before work starts, ask whether materials are included, and hire someone who answers messages fast. How quickly a pro replies before the job tells you how they'll act after it.

**What questions should customers think through before talking to professionals about their project?**
> What exactly needs doing, whether you already have the parts (TV bracket, fixture, faucet), a photo of the area, and which days work for you. With that I can give you a firm price in one message.

## 6. Reviews

Thumbtack lets you ask past customers for reviews from the "Ask for reviews" link. Send it to every founding client and anyone you've done work for. Aim for 5 reviews in week one: profiles with no reviews rarely get hired.

**Text to send with the link:**
> Hi [Name], thanks again for having me out for the [job]. I just set up my Thumbtack profile, and an honest review there would help a new local business a lot: [review link]. Takes about a minute.

Never write reviews yourself, have friends post fake ones or trade discounts for 5 stars. Thumbtack bans accounts for it.

## 7. Quick replies (save these as templates)

**First reply to a lead (send within 5 minutes; speed wins jobs):**
> Hi [Name], this is [Your name] with Quick Fix Studios. I can help with the [job]. For a standard [job] it's [$price], plus materials if needed. Could you send a photo of the area? I have openings [day] morning or [day] afternoon. Which works better?

**When they need a quote:**
> Thanks for the photo. For this job I'd quote [$price], covering [what's included]. Materials would be about [$] and I'll show you the receipt. I can do it [day/time]. Want me to lock that in?

**Follow-up if no reply after 24 hours:**
> Hi [Name], just checking in on the [job]. I still have [day] open if you'd like it. No pressure either way.

**After the job:**
> All done! Photo attached. Thanks for hiring Quick Fix Studios. If you have a minute, a review on Thumbtack helps a ton. And if anything isn't right, text me and I'll come back and fix it.

## 8. Budget and settings

- **Weekly budget:** start at $50–$75. Thumbtack charges per lead, and handyman and TV-mount leads usually cost the most. Check the cost per lead shown in each category after week one and turn off any category that costs more than ~20% of its job price.
- **Availability:** set real hours. Leads outside your hours waste money.
- **Instant book / direct leads:** turn on for the flat-rate categories (TV mount, lawn, pet waste). Leave quote-only categories as "opportunities" you choose to pay for.
- **Background check:** finish it. It adds a badge and is needed for some categories.
- **Insurance:** add the "Insured" note to the profile only once the liability policy is active (same rule as `qfs/page.html`).

## 9. Weekly routine (15 minutes, every Monday)

1. Check cost per lead and jobs won per category. Cut the worst one, raise the budget on the best one.
2. Upload new before/after photos from last week.
3. Send review links to last week's customers.
4. Answer any review on your profile, good or bad, in one polite sentence.
