# 30 Shorts / TikTok / Reels Scripts: Side Hustle Money Kit

All of these are faceless: screen recordings of the tracker on your phone, hands-only shots of items you flip, and a voiceover (yours, or a text-to-speech voice in CapCut).
Each one runs 15-35 seconds. Post 1 a day on YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Facebook/Instagram Reels at the same time.

**Link in bio:** your Shopify store (qfs1-store.myshopify.com) or the product page once it's live.
**Rules:** don't show fake earnings or say anyone will make money. Show your real numbers or label them as examples. Tag paid-product mentions as your own product where the app asks (TikTok "Branded content: your own brand").

Format: **HOOK** (first 2 seconds, on screen + spoken) / **SHOTS** / **VOICEOVER** / **CAPTION**

---

## Week 1: Flipping (stuff people already search for)

### 1. "Answer 'Is this available?' like this"
- **HOOK:** "Stop replying 'yes' to 'is this still available.'"
- **SHOTS:** Marketplace message screen → typing the script → buyer replies with a pickup time.
- **VO:** "Just saying 'yes' gets you ghosted. Say yes, then offer a pickup time: 'Yes it's available! I can do pickup today after 5 in [area]. What time works?' Now they have to commit. I keep 30 of these scripts on my phone."
- **CAPTION:** The reply that turns "is this available" into an actual pickup. #facebookmarketplace #reseller #sidehustle #flipping

### 2. "The 3x rule"
- **HOOK:** "Never pay more than this for a flip."
- **SHOTS:** Item at a garage sale → calculator → sold listings search.
- **VO:** "Look up what it actually sells for. Divide by three. That's your max, including repairs. Dresser sells for $150? You pay $50 max, all in. That's how you leave room for profit."
- **CAPTION:** Beginner flipper rule: 3x. #flipping #thriftflip #resellercommunity #sidehustle

### 3. "Lowball reply"
- **HOOK:** "How to answer a lowballer without losing the sale."
- **SHOTS:** Lowball message on screen → reply typed out.
- **VO:** "Don't get mad, counter. 'Thanks for the offer! I can't go that low. Lowest I can do is $X.' Polite, firm, and half the time they say yes."
- **CAPTION:** Lowballers are buyers in disguise. #facebookmarketplace #reselling #negotiation

### 4. "8 photos that sell"
- **HOOK:** "Your listing photos are why it's not selling."
- **SHOTS:** Fast cuts of each of the 8 shots on a real item.
- **VO:** "Hero shot in daylight. 45-degree angle. Brand label. It working. Tape measure for size. Details. Every flaw. Everything together. Eight photos, and buyers stop asking questions."
- **CAPTION:** Save this for your next listing. #marketplacetips #reseller #flipping

### 5. "Title formula"
- **HOOK:** "Nobody searches for 'Dresser.'"
- **SHOTS:** Bad title vs good title side by side.
- **VO:** "Brand plus item plus key feature plus condition. 'Solid wood 6-drawer dresser, refinished matte black, new hardware.' That shows up in way more searches."
- **CAPTION:** Steal this title formula. #facebookmarketplace #sidehustle #reselling

### 6. "Free section"
- **HOOK:** "The best flips I find cost $0."
- **SHOTS:** Marketplace Free category scroll → picking up an item → cleaned-up version.
- **VO:** "Check the Free section every day, especially the end of the month when people move. Clean it, photograph it, list it. Zero dollars in."
- **CAPTION:** Check this section daily. #flipping #curbsidefinds #sidehustle

### 7. "Scam warning: verification code"
- **HOOK:** "If a buyer asks for a code, block them."
- **SHOTS:** Example scam message (blur names) → block button.
- **VO:** "'Send me the code so I know you're real.' That code sets up a phone number in your name. Never send any code to a buyer. Ever."
- **CAPTION:** Share this with someone who sells on Marketplace. #scamalert #facebookmarketplace #staysafe
