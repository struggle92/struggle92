## The math to $10k/month

Average order of ~$28 (most buyers take the 3-pack): **$10,000 ÷ $28 ≈ 360 orders/month ≈ 12 orders/day.**
Organic TikTok converts around 0.5–1.5% of profile and link clicks, so you need **roughly 25k–70k link clicks a month.** That usually takes one or two videos breaking past 500k views.
Realistic timeline if you post 2–3 times a day: **month 1: $0–1k, month 2: $1–4k, month 3+: $10k is possible** once a format takes off. No one can guarantee this. Consistency is what makes it work.

Once organic sales cover it, put **30% of profit back into Spark Ads** (boosting your own best organic posts). That's the step that takes you from $3k to $10k+.

## Setup checklist (in order, about 2 hours total)

1. **Supplier:** in Shopify Admin → Apps, install **CJdropshipping** or **DSers**. Search "hydrocolloid pimple patch 36". Choose a listing with a 4.7★+ rating, US warehouse or 7–12 day ePacket shipping, and real buyer photos.
2. **Link the supplier listing** to the draft product's 3 variants (PP-1, PP-3, PP-6). Turn on auto-fulfill.
3. **Order one case to your own address.** You need it to film content, and you need to check quality before you sell it.
4. **Photos:** import the supplier images, then swap them for your own phone photos once the sample arrives. Real photos convert better.
5. **Payments:** Settings → Payments: turn on Shopify Payments, Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
6. **Policies:** Settings → Policies: generate Refund, Privacy, Terms and Shipping policies. A 30-day guarantee matches the product page.
7. **Remove the password page** (Online Store → Preferences) and set the product to **Active**.
8. **Theme:** use the free Dawn theme. Make the hero product the homepage feature. Put one short video above the fold.
9. **Install the free apps:** Judge.me (reviews), Shopify Email (abandoned-cart emails) and Shopify Inbox (chat).
10. **Abandoned checkout email:** Marketing → Automations → turn it on, with a 10% code in email #2.
11. **Connect the sales channels:** TikTok Shop / TikTok for Shopify, Facebook & Instagram, and Google & YouTube. All are free and sync the catalog.

## 30-day organic content plan

Post **2–3 videos a day** across TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels (same video, native upload each time). Hook in the first 1.5 seconds. Keep the product on screen within 3 seconds. Put the link in your bio, or use a TikTok Shop product tag.

### 12 video scripts (film them on your phone with your sample case)

1. **"The peel"**: macro shot of peeling a used patch off slowly, ASMR sound. Text: *"8 hours later…"* End on the white center.
2. **"POV: you have a date tomorrow"**: put a patch on at 10pm, cut to a clean peel at 7am. Text: *"tiny sticker, big save"*.
3. **"Things I stopped doing in 2026: picking"**: hand reaches for face, patch goes on instead.
4. **"Dermatologists vs. my wallet"**: price comparison: 36 patches for under $15. (Don't claim a dermatologist recommends it.)
5. **"Can you even see it?"**: zoom in on a patch worn under makeup, then reveal.
6. **"Rating every size"**: 8mm, 10mm, 12mm, each on a different spot.
7. **"What's in my nightstand"**: patch case shown alongside other bedtime items.
8. **Reply to a comment**: "Does it actually work?" → film the next morning's peel live.
9. **"Day 1 vs. day 7 of not picking"**: honest journey, no fake results.
10. **"Gift for your little sister / roommate"**: unboxing reaction.
11. **"Duet the gross peel compilation"**: stitch a viral peel video and add yours.
12. **"Why my bathroom drawer has 6 of these"**: bundle explainer for the 6-pack.

### Free traffic that isn't TikTok

- **Facebook Marketplace:** list the 3-pack locally for $25 with local pickup or shipping. Reuse the product description.
- **Reddit:** answer questions honestly in r/SkincareAddiction and r/acne. Never spam links; Reddit bans for it.
- **Creator seeding:** DM 20 micro-creators a week (5k–50k followers, skincare/teen/college niche). Offer a free 3-pack plus a 20% code with 15% commission. Template:
  > Hey [name]! Love your night-routine videos. I run a small pimple patch brand and would love to send you a free 3-pack, no strings. If you end up liking them, I'll set you up with your own code that pays 15% on every sale. Want me to ship them?

## What to track weekly (put it in your Google Sheet)

Views → link clicks → sessions → add-to-carts → orders → AOV → refunds → profit.
Kill a hook after 3 videos under 1k views. Double down on any format over 50k views: post 5 variations of it.

## Compliance (keeps your Shopify payouts and TikTok account safe)

- Never say "cures acne", "treats acne" or "dermatologist recommended" unless it's true and documented.
- No fake reviews and no fake before/after photos. Only use real customer photos, with their permission.
- Fake countdown timers and "only 3 left" scarcity count as deceptive practices. Don't use them.
- Ship-time honesty: say 7–12 days if that's what it is. Late orders turn into chargebacks.
- Disclose any paid creator content with #ad.
